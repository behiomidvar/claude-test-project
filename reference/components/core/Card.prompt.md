The base surface for grouped content — profiles, prompts, settings rows. Generously rounded with a soft, warm-tinted shadow.

```jsx
<Card>Standard raised card</Card>
<Card variant="gradient">The one hero moment</Card>   {/* use sparingly */}
<Card variant="cool">On a dark cool surface</Card>
<Card interactive onClick={...}>Tappable, lifts on hover</Card>
```

Variants: `raised | flat | cool | gradient`. Props: `interactive`, `pad` (`sm|md|lg`).
