Minimum artwork height is the figure on the scope line at the top of this card — stated once, there. Below it there is no lockup: use the دلتاک پلاس badge pill. Why 120 and not the measured legibility floor is in readme.md, PLUS LOCKUP SPEC.

Do

  • Wordmark leads, icon at its right — the RTL reading start.
  • The plus stays superscript, at its drawn size and position: 28 × 28 at x 0–28, its baseline 16.18u above the type's. That relationship is the artwork.
  • The icon is height-matched to the 44.18u type block and centred against it — the block is the plus top to the type baseline, not the type ink alone.
  • Vertical: mark, gap, wordmark — all centred on the wordmark's bbox axis, x 96.
  • Scale by height only; move the lockup as one unit.

Never

  • Never build a compact Plus lockup. No Plus surface carries a lockup in a top bar, so it would have no consumer. The absence is a decision.
  • Never set the tagline under it. دل به دل راه داره is about the product, not a subscription tier, and it is restricted to splash, onboarding and About.
  • Never reseat the plus on the type baseline, resize it against cap-height, or set it apart — those were options B and C and they were not chosen.
  • Never carry #F24C9A into dark. Never resize icon and type independently.
  • Never use it in place of the standard lockup outside the paywall hero.
