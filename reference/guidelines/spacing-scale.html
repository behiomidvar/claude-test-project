The ramp — 4px base grid

Which spacing does what

Six semantic role tokens sit on top of the raw ramp. Reach for the role, not the number — the role can be re-tuned system-wide, a raw --space-N cannot. Raw steps stay legal for genuine one-offs (an optical nudge, a decorative offset) where no role applies.

--gutter-screenScreen horizontal padding. The left/right margin every screen's content sits inside. Never applied to the top/bottom.
--gap-sectionBetween major sections of a screen — the largest routine gap, marking a change of subject.
--pad-cardA card's interior padding, on all four sides. Content never touches the card edge.
--gap-cardBetween sibling cards or list rows. Tighter than a section gap — related items, not separate subjects.
--gap-stackVertical rhythm between stacked form fields, each with its own label. Roomy enough that labels bind to the field below them.
--gap-inlineBetween elements inside one row — chips, icon and label, buttons in a toolbar. The tightest routine gap.
