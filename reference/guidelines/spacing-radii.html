The raw ramp — geometry only

These steps carry no role. Reach for a role token below; a raw step is for a genuine one-off (a nested inner corner, a decorative shape) where no role applies.

Which radius does what

Five role tokens, each with exactly one value. Reach for the role, never the raw step.

--radius-buttonButton and IconButton. Softer than a field so an action reads as pressable, not typeable.
چی صدات کنیم؟
--radius-inputText field, textarea, select trigger, OTP box. The tightest role — a field is a container for content, not a shape.
یک کارت

هر سطح کارت‌مانند همین گردی را دارد.

--radius-cardCard and every card-like surface — plan cards, prompt cards, permission cards, list panels.
کتابقهوهٔ دستی
--radius-chipChips, badges, pills — fully round. Also the sheet grab-handle and other capsule shapes.
یک شیت
--radius-sheetBottom sheets, dialogs, large overlay surfaces. The most generous role — a sheet is a new plane, not a card.
این یکی از طرف توستو این از طرف اوست
--radius-bubble-meSpeech bubbles: rounded on three corners, tight on the tail corner. -me and -them mirror each other, and the pair is already RTL-correct.
