The icon artwork carries 4.5u of built-in padding per side (a 48-unit frame around 36 units of ink, scaled here to a 36u frame around 27u of ink). All alignment math keys to the ink box, never the frame — measuring from the frame is what inflated the lockup.

Do

  • Wordmark leads, icon at its right — the RTL reading start.
  • Compact: bottom alignment only — the icon ink's bottom edge on the wordmark's ink baseline. The icon's 27u of ink stands taller than the 24u wordmark and overhangs it at the top; that is correct.
  • Full: icon height-matched and centred against the wordmark + tagline block.

Never

  • Baseline-align the Full lockup.
  • Centre or top-align the Compact lockup.
  • Scale the icon down to the wordmark's 24u height — the withdrawn 142 × 24 build did this and the type then overpowered the mark.

Compact in the persistent top bar

Artwork fixed at 36px tall (1u = 1px → 152 × 36), centred; the container carries no height requirement.
