400–600 — flat usage

--brand-purple-500

700–900 — fills & borders

--brand-indigo-800

100–300 — on dark fills

--brand-indigo-200

متن و آیکون روی همان پرکردگی‌های ۷۰۰–۹۰۰.

Usage. 500 is the base value · 400–600 default flat usage · 700–900 tinted fills, hover and borders on light surfaces · 100–300 text and icons on top of those fills. Reach for a ramp step before color-mix() or a hand-lightened hex. Ramps are theme-independent; per-theme tints stay semantic.
