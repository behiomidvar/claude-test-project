One shape, two meanings. The warm arm (apricot→magenta) is a heart reaching out — دل. The cool arm (indigo→violet) is the words coming back. The three dots are a typing indicator: someone is about to speak. The logo keeps both gradient families in both themes — it is the one place warm and cool meet, and never re-tints per theme (intentional exception to the warm-light / cool-dark accent rule).
